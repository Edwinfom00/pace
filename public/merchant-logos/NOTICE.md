# Merchant mark notice

These local SVGs are displayed only to identify recognised merchants in transaction activity.

- The curated catalog uses Simple Icons 16.31.0 as its source for the marked brands. Simple Icons is distributed under CC0, while individual marks can have their own license terms and brand-guideline requirements: https://simpleicons.org/
- `mtn.svg` is sourced from MTN's published design-system asset: https://onlinecms.mtn.co.za/sites/default/files/design-system%2C%20vivid%2C%20images%2C%20icons/brand/mtn_logo_lg-sunshine.svg
- `yango.svg` is the asset linked from Yango's public site: https://cdnrhkgfkkpupuotntfj.svc.cdn.yandex.net/yangologo.svg
- `amazon.svg` is sourced from Wikimedia Commons' Amazon 2024 SVG: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_2024.svg

All trade names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Review the relevant brand guidelines before using these assets outside a transaction-identification context.

The Cameroon-local entries that use initials or service glyphs are intentionally neutral transaction-identification badges, not recreated corporate logos.
