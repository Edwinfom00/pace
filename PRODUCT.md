# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pace serves people and shared households who need a calm, trustworthy place to understand spending, plan for goals, and make money decisions together. They commonly arrive with an immediate task—reviewing a transaction, importing a statement, or getting oriented to their finances—and need the interface to stay clear under that cognitive load.

## Product Purpose

Pace turns household financial data into an understandable, reviewable workspace for tracking money, planning goals, handling recurring payments, and receiving safe, scoped guidance from Pace. Success means users can understand what happened, decide what to do next, and remain in control of every material financial change.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, and encouraging. Pace should make financial work feel less intimidating without trivialising it: warm enough to support a household, precise enough to earn trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, default component-library pages, ornamental gradients, and any interface that substitutes decoration for clarity. Do not use exaggerated fintech drama, dense trading-terminal visual language, or overly playful money metaphors.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next financial decision easy to understand.
2. Earn trust through restraint, clear hierarchy, and explicit state.
3. Keep users in control of consequential actions.
4. Support shared financial life without making it feel complicated.
5. Let calm, purposeful guidance take precedence over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-first use, logical landmarks and heading structure, visible focus states, readable contrast, and responsive layouts that preserve touch targets and reading order. Respect reduced-motion preferences when motion is introduced.
